About PG Finder

PG Finder is a student project designed to help people explore paying guest accommodations and shared rooms.

Visitors can browse listings and filter them by location, room type, and monthly rent.

Registered users can log in, save favorite rooms, and submit new accommodation listings.

The project uses HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js, Express, and Oracle Database.
